The show's MadMapper project

The show keeps one MadMapper project: patching and the bundle start from it. After changing it in MadMapper, upload it again (the patched copy is fine: patching again sets its MIDI triggers and keeps everything else). The last 5 projects it replaced are kept.

Upload a MadMapper 5 .mad, or a MadMapper 6 .madproject folder zipped (Finder: Compress). A MadMapper 6 folder can hold its media, so to send less, upload only the .mad inside it (Show Package Contents); its patched copy comes back under the same name, to put into a copy of the folder in place of the original.

    Patch it

    Every cue (in MadMapper 6, every timeline) named like an effect gets a MIDI trigger on that effect's note and channel: the notes the generated MIDI files play. You get a patched copy to download; the show's project is not changed. Save and close the project in MadMapper first.

    Build

    Bundle: extract the whole zip, then open the .rpp inside it. Opened from inside the zip, REAPER finds none of its media.
